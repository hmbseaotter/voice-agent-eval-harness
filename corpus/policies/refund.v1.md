# Refund policy — `refund.v1`

**Applies to:** all ticket purchases made through Verso Tickets.
**Version:** 1. **Effective:** 2027-01-01.

Clause identifiers are stable. A `POLICY` event in a transcript cites this document and clause as
`refund.v1 § <clause>`, and the quoted text in that event must equal the clause text below after
collapsing runs of whitespace to a single space. Not byte-for-byte: both documents wrap, and they
wrap at different points. That correspondence is what makes "the agent misstated a clause it
retrieved" a checkable claim rather than an impression.

---

## 1. Scope

**1.1** This policy governs refunds requested by the ticket holder. Refunds arising from an event
being canceled or rescheduled by the promoter are governed by section 5 and are not subject to the
windows in section 2.

**1.2** Every ticket is sold with a booking fee. The booking fee is stated separately at checkout.

## 2. Refund windows

**2.1** "A full refund of the ticket price is available where the request is made more than 14 days
before the scheduled door time."

**2.2** "Where the request is made between 14 days and 72 hours before the scheduled door time, 50%
of the ticket price is refundable."

**2.3** "No refund of the ticket price is available where the request is made within 72 hours of the
scheduled door time."

**2.4** "The booking fee is non-refundable under this section, including where a full refund of the
ticket price is due."

## 3. Method and timing

**3.1** "A refund is issued to the original payment method. Where the original method is no longer
valid, the refund is held until the account holder supplies a replacement."

**3.2** "Card refunds are submitted to Cardinal Pay within one business day and reach the
cardholder's statement within five business days of submission."

**3.3** "Partial refunds against a split-tender payment are submitted to each instrument in the
proportion originally charged."

## 4. Eligibility

**4.1** "A refund cannot be issued against a booking whose tickets have been transferred to another
named holder. The transfer must be reversed by the current holder before a refund can be requested."

**4.2** "A booking that has been exchanged retains the refund window of the *original* purchase, not
of the event it was exchanged into."

**4.3** "Where any ticket on a booking has been scanned at the venue, no ticket on that booking is
refundable."

## 5. Promoter cancellation and rescheduling

**5.1** "Where an event is canceled by the promoter, the ticket price and the booking fee are both
refunded in full, automatically, within ten business days. No request is required."

**5.2** "Where an event is rescheduled by the promoter, tickets remain valid for the new date. A
holder who cannot attend the new date may request a full refund of the ticket price and the booking
fee within 14 days of the reschedule being announced."
