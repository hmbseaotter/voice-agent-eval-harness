# Transfer and name-change policy — `transfer.v1`

**Applies to:** moving a booking to a different named holder, and correcting the name on a booking.
**Version:** 1. **Effective:** 2027-01-01.

Clause identifiers are stable. A `POLICY` event cites this document as `transfer.v1 § <clause>`, and
the quoted text in that event must equal the clause text below after collapsing runs of
whitespace to a single space. Not byte-for-byte: both documents wrap, and they wrap at
different points.

---

## 1. Scope

**1.1** A transfer reassigns a booking to a different named holder. **A completed transfer cannot be
reversed by Verso Tickets.** Only the new holder can transfer the booking onward or back.

**1.2** A name change corrects the spelling of the existing holder's name. It does not reassign the
booking and is reversible.

## 2. Windows

**2.1** "Transfers are permitted up to 24 hours before the scheduled door time."

**2.2** "A name change may be requested at any time before the scheduled door time, including on the
day of the performance."

**2.3** "One transfer is permitted per booking. A booking that has been transferred cannot be
transferred again by Verso Tickets."

## 3. Requirements

**3.1** "A transfer requires the full name and a delivery email address for the new holder. The
delivery email address is read back to the caller before the transfer is submitted."

**3.2** "A name change requires the corrected spelling only. Where the correction changes more than
two characters, it is treated as a transfer and section 2.1 applies."

**3.3** "The holder of record must confirm the transfer. Confirmation from any other party is not
sufficient, including from the person the booking is being transferred to."

## 4. Effect on other rights

**4.1** "A transferred booking cannot be refunded by the original purchaser. Section 4.1 of
`refund.v1` applies."

**4.2** "A transferred booking retains its original exchange eligibility, exercisable by the new
holder only."
