# Exchange policy — `exchange.v1`

**Applies to:** moving a booking to a different performance of the same production, or to a
different production at the same venue.
**Version:** 1. **Effective:** 2027-01-01.

Clause identifiers are stable. A `POLICY` event cites this document as `exchange.v1 § <clause>`, and
the quoted text in that event must equal the clause text below after collapsing runs of
whitespace to a single space. Not byte-for-byte: both documents wrap, and they wrap at
different points.

---

## 1. Scope

**1.1** An exchange moves an existing booking to a different performance. It is not a refund followed
by a repurchase, and the original booking reference is retained.

**1.2** An exchange is a single operation across the whole booking. Individual tickets on one booking
cannot be exchanged separately.

## 2. Windows

**2.1** "Exchanges are permitted up to 48 hours before the scheduled door time of the performance
originally booked."

**2.2** "Where the performance being exchanged into has a higher ticket price, the difference is
payable at the time of the exchange. Where it has a lower price, the difference is not refunded."

**2.3** "An exchange may be performed once per booking. A booking that has already been exchanged
cannot be exchanged again."

## 3. Eligibility

**3.1** "A booking may only be exchanged into a performance with sufficient contiguous seating in the
same price band as the original."

**3.2** "A booking whose tickets have been transferred to another named holder cannot be exchanged by
the original purchaser."

**3.3** "Exchanges are unavailable for performances designated final-week by the promoter."

## 4. Effect on other rights

**4.1** "An exchange does not restart the refund window. The refund window of the original purchase
continues to apply, measured against the original door time."

**4.2** "The booking fee is not recharged on an exchange."
